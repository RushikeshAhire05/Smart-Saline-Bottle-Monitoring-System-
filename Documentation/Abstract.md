In modern healthcare environments, continuous monitoring of patients is essential to 
ensure their safety and proper treatment. One of the most common yet critical tasks in 
hospitals is monitoring the saline level of intravenous ( IV) bottles. Traditionally, this 
process is carried out manually by nurses or hospital staff, which can sometimes lead to 
delays, especially in busy hospital settings. If the saline bottle becomes empty and is not 
replaced on time, it may result in serious complications for the patient, including the risk 
of blood backflow. This highlights the need for an efficient and reliable monitoring system. 
The I o T based hospital management system for patient monitoring is 
designed as a simple and effective solution to address this issue using Internet of 
Things (IoT) technology. The system focuses on automating the process of saline level 
monitoring and providing timely alerts to medical staff. It uses a load cell (weight sensor) 
to continuously measure the weight of the saline bottle placed on the stand. As the saline 
level decreases, the weight of the bottle reduces accordingly. This change is detected by 
the sensor and processed using an ESP32 microcontroller. 
The ESP32 plays a key role in the system by collecting sensor data and transmitting 
it over a Wi- Fi network. The data is then displayed on a mobile application, allowing 
healthcare staff to monitor saline levels in real time without being physically present near 
the patient.  In addition to remote monitoring, the system also includes a buzzer that 
gets activated when the saline level falls below a predefined threshold. This provides an 
immediate local alert, ensuring that necessary action can be taken without delay. 
The proposed system offers several advantages, including reduced manual effort, im
proved accuracy in monitoring, and enhanced patient safety. It is cost-effective, easy to 
install, and can be implemented in hospitals, clinics, and even for home patient care. 
By integrating sensor technology with Io T communication, the project demonstrates a 
practical approach to solving a real-world healthcare problem. 
In conclusion, the Smart Saline Bottle Stand provides a reliable and 
efficient method for monitoring I V fluid levels. It minimizes human error, ensures 
timely inter- vention, and contributes to better healthcare management. This project 
highlights how modern technology can be used to improve existing medical practices 
and create safer environments for patients.
